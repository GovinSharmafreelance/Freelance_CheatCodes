### 1. 🎯 Expert Positioning Statement

**What it does:** Generates 5 powerful positioning statements that lead with your client's result — not your job title.

    You are a veteran freelance strategist with 10+ years of experience.

    I am a freelance [YOUR ROLE] who helps [TARGET CLIENT]
    achieve [SPECIFIC OUTCOME].

    Write me 5 expert positioning statements I can use
    across my profiles and pitches. Each should:
    - Be under 2 sentences
    - Lead with the client's result, not my job title
    - Sound confident and specific
    - Avoid buzzwords like 'passionate' or 'dedicated'

    Then pick the strongest one and explain why it works.

---

### 2. ✍️ Winning Proposal Hook

**What it does:** Writes a proposal opening so good the client stops scrolling and reads every word.

    You are a freelance business consultant with 10+ years
    of experience helping freelancers win better clients.

    I'm applying for a [PROJECT TYPE] job posted by a client
    who needs [DESCRIBE CLIENT'S NEED].

    Write a winning proposal that:
    - Opens with a hook proving I read the brief
    - Immediately addresses their core problem
    - Proposes a clear confident solution
    - Includes one relevant result that builds credibility
    - Closes with a low-friction CTA

    Length: 150-200 words. Tone: confident, zero fluff.

---

### 3. 💰 Price Increase Email

**What it does:** Writes the exact email to raise your rates without losing clients or apologizing.

    You are a freelance business advisor with 10+ years
    in the industry.

    I've been charging [CURRENT RATE] for [YOUR SERVICE]
    and want to raise my rates to [TARGET RATE].

    Write me:
    1. The email to announce this to existing clients
    2. How to present higher rates to new clients
       without apologizing
    3. How to handle pushback from clients who say
       'you used to charge less'

    Tone: confident, warm, professional.
